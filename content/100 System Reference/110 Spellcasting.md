## Casting A Spell
Spells come in all shapes and sizes, and cover all manner of effects (*i.e. teleportation, fireballs, invisibility* ). Each spell will denote the (*often unique*) result of successfully casting it, however, there are a few features that all spells have in common:

- **Source**: From where the spell originates (*i.e. Divine*)
- **Type**: The elemental form the spell takes (*i.e. ==Thunder==*)
- **Delivery**: The manner with which the spell is cast (*i.e. Verbal)*
- **Cost**: The amount of MP required when attempting to cast the spell

Casting a spell during combat also requires a main action (*unless explicitly noted*).

#### Spellcasting Die
Unlike martial combatants, the potency of a spellcaster's arsenal is not dependent on the weapon (*spell*) they choose, instead, it is drawn from their innate power and magical connections. Each PC has their own independent proficiency die to be used each time they cast a spell.

> [!tip] Not having a spellcasting die does not preclude a PC from casting spells, it just makes it harder to successfully do so. Providing a PC has learned the spell they wish to cast, and has enough MP to do so, a simple d20 check (*with no proficiency die*) will suffice.

#### Semantic & Verbal
Some spells may have a semantic or verbal delivery, which means they rely on the spellcaster to move their hands in a particular way (semantic) or speak a collection of mystical phrases (verbal). If the PC is unable to do such things (*i.e. their arms are tied behind their back*), then they will be unable to cast these types of spells.

By this nature, semantic spells require a PC to have one or two hands free of other equipment (*i.e. weapons*) to cast successfully.

> [!warning] Some of the most powerful spells require both semantic and verbal delivery.

## Concentration
If a PC has cast a spell that requires concentration (*usually those that persist*), they must remain steadfast in the face of oncoming danger, or else risk losing their grip on the magical entity they have carefully put in place.

When a PC who is concentrating is hit with an attack (*or encounters a trap that requires them to move to avoid harm*), resolve the action as normal, then additionally compare the attack value against the PC's Willpower. Regardless of whether the attack succeeded, if the result beats the Willpower of the PC, their concentration is broken, and the spell dissipates.

## Arcane Focus
Arcane focuses are remarkable pieces of equipment that channel the magical potential of a PC, allowing them to cast greater and more magnificent spells. Wielding an arcane focus conveys the following benefits to a PC:
- Semantic or verbal spellcasting are no longer required (providing the PC can gesture with their focus)
- The PC can utilise stunts (*like weapons*) whilst casting spells through their focus, the manner of which varies based on their implement.
#### Attunement
In order to use an arcane focus, a PC must attune to it. This takes significant patience (*i.e. a downtime action*), but once attuned, the focus will operate flawlessly. However, if a PC cannot wield their focus for any reason (*i.e. it is stolen from them*) then they are unable to cast any spells until it is recovered (*or the PC takes the time to attune to a different focus instead*). A PC may only be attuned to one focus at a time.

## Spell Components
Spell components are consumable ingredients that, when used, apply additional effects onto a PC's spells. Some may cause the damage type to change, whilst others may increase a spell's reach. A PC can choose to use a component alongside casting a spell providing they have a ==minor action== to do so.
#### Runes
Runes are ancient stones that have the capacity to hold latent magical energy. A PC in possession of a rune can choose to utilise it's power instead of their own. Expending a rune grants the user enough power to cast any spell they know, providing the spell being cast is of the same type as the rune's energy (*i.e. ==fire, cold, psychic==*).

> [!warning] Runes are an unstable form of storage for magical energy, caution should be taken when handling them to avoid mishaps or devastating chain reactions.
#### Scrolls
Scrolls are once step beyond runes. They are parchments of written word, that when spoken, allow a PC to cast a particular spell. Each scroll is imbued with enough magical energy to cast the dictated spell, after which, the scroll disintegrates (*much like a rune*), thus, a PC reading the scroll need not the knowledge or the skill to cast any spells to enact powerful effects.
